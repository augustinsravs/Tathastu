A Craft Carried Through Generations

In the areca groves of our region, the palm sheds a broad, tough leaf every few weeks. For generations it was swept aside. Our artisans saw a plate in it.

  • Leaves are collected only after they fall naturally — no tree is ever cut.
  • Sun-dried, then soaked and scrubbed clean in fresh running water.
  • Pressed at 120°C in steel dies — heat and pressure alone bind the fibres.
  • Hand-inspected, UV-treated and packed in food-grade sleeves.
