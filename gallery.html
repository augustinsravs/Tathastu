Our unit, our people

Everything Happens Under One Roof

We don't outsource. Leaves arrive at our unit, and finished cartons leave it — which is how we keep quality identical across every batch and pricing honest for every customer.

  • Six pressing stations running through the season.
  • Hand inspection at three separate points before packing.
  • Fair, direct wages paid to the tribal families who collect and press.
  • Visitors welcome — come see the unit before placing a large order.
